Image Cropper

Crop images freely or to a fixed aspect ratio like 1:1, 4:5 or 16:9.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Add a JPG, PNG or WEBP image.
  2. Pick Free or a fixed ratio, then drag the box or its corners over the part you want.
  3. Tap Crop, check the preview and download.

About Image Cropper

The Image Cropper cuts your photo down to the part that matters. Drag the crop box with a mouse or your finger, lock it to a common shape for Instagram, YouTube or print, and see the exact crop size in pixels as you go. The result keeps the original resolution of the area you chose and is saved in the same format as your original.

FAQs

Does cropping reduce quality?

No. Cropping only removes pixels; the area you keep stays at its original resolution. JPG and WEBP are saved again at high quality.

Which aspect ratio should I use?

Square 1:1 or Portrait 4:5 for Instagram posts, Landscape 16:9 for YouTube and screens, and 4:3 or 3:4 for prints and most phone photos.

Can I move the box with a keyboard?

Yes. Click or tab to the image and use the arrow keys to move the crop box; hold Shift for bigger steps.
