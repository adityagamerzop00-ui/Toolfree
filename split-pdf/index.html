Split PDF

Extract pages, or split a PDF into single pages or chunks.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Only the open-source processing engine is downloaded when the tool opens.

How to use

  1. Add one PDF, up to 50 MB.
  2. Choose Extract pages (e.g. 1-3,5), Every page separately, or Chunks of N pages.
  3. Tap Split PDF and download your files.

About Split PDF

Split PDF pulls out just the pages you need, or breaks a long document into single pages or equal parts. Pages are copied without any loss of quality. Your PDF is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

FAQs

How do I write page ranges?

Separate pages and ranges with commas: 1-3,5 gives pages 1, 2, 3 and 5. 7- means page 7 to the end.

What if the last chunk is short?

With Chunks of N pages, the final file holds whatever pages are left, so a 10-page PDF in chunks of 3 gives files of 3, 3, 3 and 1 pages.

Why doesn't Download all save every file?

Some browsers ask before saving several files at once. Allow it, or tap each file's own Download button.
