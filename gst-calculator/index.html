GST Calculator

Add or remove GST at 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% or any rate, with the CGST and SGST split.

Everything is worked out in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere or saved.

How to use

  1. Choose Add GST or Remove GST.
  2. Tap a rate, or Custom to type your own, and enter the amount.
  3. Read the net, GST, CGST, SGST and gross amounts.

About GST Calculator

GST Calculator adds tax to a price, or takes it out of a GST-inclusive price using Net = Gross ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). For sales within a state, the GST splits equally into CGST and SGST. Everything is worked out in your browser as you type; nothing is sent anywhere or saved.

FAQs

How do I remove GST from a price?

Choose Remove GST and enter the inclusive price. ₹1,180 at 18% gives ₹1,000 net.

What about IGST?

For sales between states, IGST equals the full GST amount shown.

Which rate applies to my item?

Rates depend on the goods or service; check the current GST rate notified for it.
