Frequently asked questions

Is SugoiTools really free?

Yes. Every tool is free to use with no sign-up, no watermark and no daily limit.

Are my files uploaded?

No. Your files are processed on your own device, inside your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.

Is there a file size limit?

Image tools take files up to 20 MB each, PDF tools up to 50 MB and video tools up to 500 MB. Very large files can be slow on older phones.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. SugoiTools works in modern browsers on iPhone, Android, tablets and computers.

Which formats are supported?

Image tools open and save JPG, PNG and WEBP. Video tools open MP4, MOV, WEBM and MKV and save MP4 (or WebM, MOV and audio in the converter). PDF tools work with standard PDF files.
