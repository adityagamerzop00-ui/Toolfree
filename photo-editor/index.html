Photo Editor

Adjust brightness, contrast and colour, add filters, borders and a watermark.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded.

How to use

  1. Add a JPG, PNG or WEBP image.
  2. Adjust brightness, contrast and saturation, or tap a filter; add a frame or watermark if you like.
  3. Choose JPG or PNG and download the edited photo.

About Photo Editor

The Photo Editor covers the quick fixes most photos need: lighten a dark shot, add punch with contrast and saturation, or give it a look with one-tap filters such as Vintage, Warm or Vivid. Add a solid, rounded or Polaroid-style frame and a text watermark in any corner. The preview updates as you go and everything happens in your browser.

FAQs

Can it remove the background?

Background removal isn't included yet — free browser-only options are too heavy for phones. It's planned for a later version.

Why choose PNG instead of JPG?

PNG keeps transparency, including the corners of a Rounded frame, and is lossless. JPG files are much smaller for photos.

Will the watermark cover my photo?

It sits inside the photo at the position you choose. Lower the opacity to make it subtle, or pick a corner to keep it out of the way.
