Bio Generator

Eight short bios at a time for Instagram, X, LinkedIn or YouTube.

Everything runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved.

How to use

  1. Type what you do and a few interests.
  2. Pick a tone and a platform, then tap Generate.
  3. Copy a bio you like; red means it is over the limit.

About Bio Generator

Fills about 20 bio templates with your details, with emoji for Fun and Casual, and counts characters against the platform's limit.

FAQs

Where do the ideas come from?

Ideas come from proven templates on your device, so they're instant and private.

What are the limits?

Instagram 150, X 160, LinkedIn headline 220, YouTube 1,000 characters.

Do I need an account?

No. It's free with no sign-up, and nothing you type leaves your browser.
