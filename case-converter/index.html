About Text Case Converter

The Text Case Converter rewrites text as UPPER, lower, Title or Sentence case, the camelCase, PascalCase, snake_case and kebab-case styles programmers use, and playful aLtErNaTe and iNVERSE. Every version updates as you type, with a live character count. It runs entirely in your browser, with nothing sent anywhere or saved.

FAQs

What is camelCase?

Words joined with no spaces, each capitalised except the first, like helloWorldExample. PascalCase capitalises the first too.

Does Title Case skip small words?

No. It capitalises every word, which is the simplest style and works for most headings.

Does it handle hyphens and underscores?

Yes. For the code styles, spaces, hyphens and underscores all count as word breaks.
