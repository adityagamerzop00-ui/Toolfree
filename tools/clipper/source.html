        FAQ

        Questions, answered

        Is it really free?

        Yes. Every feature is free to use. There is no signup, no paid plan and no hidden limit.

        Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

        No. Your video is processed on your own device, inside the browser. Nothing is uploaded. Auto captions download a speech model once (about 80 to 150 MB), and your audio still stays on your device.

        Which browser works best?

        Chrome on a desktop computer or an Android phone works best. Edge works too. Some other browsers can't record or export video yet.

        Which video formats are supported?

        MP4 and WebM work best, and MOV works in most browsers. Clips save as MP4 (best for phones) or WebM.

        Can it make Hindi captions?

        Yes. In Style, choose Auto captions and pick Hindi. For Hinglish in Roman letters, pick English. You can also type captions or load an SRT/VTT file.

        Why is export slow on my phone?

        Every clip is rendered on your device, and phones have less power than computers. Use the Fast render engine, pick Medium or Small quality, and keep this tab open and in front until it finishes.

        Is there a watermark?

        No. Your clips never get a watermark.
