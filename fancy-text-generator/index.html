Fancy Text Generator

Turn plain text into bold, script, bubble and other Unicode styles to copy.

Everything runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved.

How to use

  1. Type your text in the box.
  2. Browse the 15 styles as they update.
  3. Tap Copy next to one and paste it anywhere.

About Fancy Text Generator

Fancy Text Generator swaps your letters for look-alike Unicode characters, so the style survives copy and paste where fonts can't be changed. Your text stays in your browser and is never uploaded.

FAQs

Will it work on Instagram, X and TikTok?

Yes, in most places that accept text. A few apps or older devices may show boxes for some styles.

Why are some characters unchanged?

Unicode has no styled version of every symbol or accented letter, so those stay as they are.

Is fancy text bad for accessibility?

Screen readers may read it oddly, so keep it for short names and headings.
