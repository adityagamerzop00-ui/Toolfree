Video Cropper

Crop the frame of a video to any shape, right in your browser.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Only the open-source processing engine is downloaded when the tool opens.

How to use

  1. Add one MP4, MOV, WEBM or MKV video, up to 500 MB.
  2. Pick a ratio, then drag the box and its corners over the area to keep.
  3. Tap Crop video and download the cropped MP4.

About Video Cropper

Video Cropper cuts away the edges of the picture and keeps the area inside the box, at full resolution. It works with a mouse or a finger. Your video is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

FAQs

Does cropping lower the quality?

The kept area stays at its original resolution. The video is re-encoded, so quality stays close to the original.

Why do the numbers change by 2?

Video encoders need even sizes, so the crop is rounded to even pixel values.

Can I crop for Instagram or TikTok?

Yes. Use 1:1 or 4:5 for feed posts and 9:16 for Reels, Stories and TikTok.
