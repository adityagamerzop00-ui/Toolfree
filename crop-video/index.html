Video Cropper

Crop the frame of a video to any shape, right in your browser.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Only the open-source processing engine is downloaded when the tool opens.

How to use

  1. Add one MP4, MOV, WEBM or MKV video, up to 500 MB.
  2. Pick a ratio, then drag the box and its corners over the area to keep.
  3. Tap Crop video and download the cropped MP4.

About Video Cropper

Video Cropper cuts away the edges of the picture and keeps the area inside the box, at full resolution. It works with a mouse or a finger. Your video is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

Video Cropper guide

Crop a video for Instagram Reels and TikTok

Pick 9:16 and drag the box over the part of a wide video you want to keep, such as the speaker's face. The kept area stays at its original resolution, so a 1080p landscape clip becomes a sharp vertical video without black bars.

Square and 4:5 posts

Feed posts look biggest on screen at 4:5, and 1:1 suits profile grids and ads. Lock the ratio first, then move the box; the crop size in pixels updates as you drag so you know the exact output.

Remove black bars, watermarks at the edge or unwanted objects

Choose Free and pull the box edges in until the bars or the unwanted corner are outside it. This is the fastest way to clean up a screen recording or a video shot with the wrong framing.

FAQs

Does cropping lower the quality?

The kept area stays at its original resolution. The video is re-encoded, so quality stays close to the original.

Why do the numbers change by 2?

Video encoders need even sizes, so the crop is rounded to even pixel values.

Can I crop for Instagram or TikTok?

Yes. Use 1:1 or 4:5 for feed posts and 9:16 for Reels, Stories and TikTok.

Is there a watermark on the cropped video?

No. The video is free to download with no watermark and no sign-up.

Can I crop a video on my phone?

Yes. It works in Chrome on Android and Safari on iPhone. Drag the box with your finger; very long videos are faster on a computer.

What is the difference between cropping and resizing?

Cropping cuts away the edges and keeps part of the picture. Resizing keeps the whole picture and changes its pixel size. Use Resize Video when you need a smaller file without cutting anything.
