Resize a photo to 20 KB for a form

Exam, job and government forms often want a photo or signature under 20 KB, sometimes also between a minimum and maximum size. A phone photo is usually 2 to 5 MB, so you need two quick steps: resize, then compress.

Open Image Resizer

Step 1: resize to the right pixels

  1. Check the form's rules for the photo size in pixels (or in cm or mm).
  2. Open the Image Resizer, add your photo and type that width and height. The Passport preset makes 413 × 531 pixels (35 × 45 mm at 300 DPI); always check what your form asks for.
  3. Download the resized photo.

Step 2: compress under 20 KB

  1. Open the Image Compressor and add the resized photo.
  2. Choose JPEG and lower the quality slider. A small passport-size photo often reaches 20 KB at a quality of around 50 to 70.
  3. Check the new size shown. If it is still above 20 KB, lower the quality a little more, or set a smaller maximum width. Then download.

Tips

  • Crop to the face and shoulders first with the Image Cropper, so the file has less to store.
  • If the form also has a minimum size (for example 10 KB), don't push the quality too low.
  • A signature on white paper compresses very well. Crop it tightly before you resize.
  • Most forms want JPG. Use the Image Converter if your photo is PNG or WEBP.

FAQs

Why is my photo still over 20 KB?

It is probably still too large in pixels. Resize it to the size your form asks for first, then compress. Lowering quality alone rarely gets a big phone photo to 20 KB.

Will my photo look blurry?

At passport size and a quality of 50 to 70 it usually looks fine. Check the preview before you download.

Is my photo uploaded?

No. Resizing and compressing happen in your browser, and the photo never leaves your device.
