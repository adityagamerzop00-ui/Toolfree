Compress a PDF to 100 KB

Many government, exam and job portals only accept PDFs under 100 KB. Here is the quickest way to get a scanned document under that limit for free, right in your phone's browser.

Open Compress PDF

Steps

  1. Open the Compress PDF tool and add your PDF (up to 50 MB).
  2. Choose Strong compression and tap Compress PDF. Strong redraws each page at 80 DPI, the smallest setting.
  3. Check the new size shown. If it is under 100 KB, download it and upload it to your form.

Still over 100 KB?

  • Remove pages you don't need: use Split PDF or Organize PDF to keep only the pages the form asks for, then compress again.
  • Scan in black and white or greyscale if your scanner app allows it. Colour scans are much heavier.
  • Crop the white margins in your scanner app before you make the PDF.
  • A 1-page scan usually fits in 100 KB at Strong. Many pages often won't, so check whether the form lets you upload pages separately.

Good to know

  • Compress PDF turns each page into an image, so text can't be selected afterwards. That is fine for scans and certificates, but keep your original file.
  • Your PDF is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

FAQs

Can every PDF be compressed to 100 KB?

No. A one or two page scan usually can at Strong compression. Long documents or very detailed colour pages may stay above 100 KB, so remove extra pages first.

Which compression level should I use for 100 KB?

Start with Strong. Low and Medium keep more detail, which is better for limits like 500 KB or 1 MB.

Is it safe for ID documents?

Yes. The file is compressed inside your browser and is never uploaded to a server.
