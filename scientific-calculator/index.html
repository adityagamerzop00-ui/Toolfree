Scientific Calculator

A scientific calculator with trig, logs, powers and roots, by tap or keyboard.

Everything is worked out in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere or saved.

How to use

  1. Tap the keys or type on your keyboard.
  2. Switch DEG or RAD for trig functions.
  3. Read the live answer, then press = or Enter.

About Scientific Calculator

Scientific Calculator follows the usual order of operations: brackets, powers, then × and ÷, then + and −. It supports sin, cos, tan, log, ln, square roots, powers, percent, π and e, and multiplies implicitly, so 2π means 2 × π. Everything is worked out in your browser as you type; nothing is sent anywhere or saved.

FAQs

What does % do?

It divides the number before it by 100, so 50% is 0.5.

Is log base 10?

Yes. log is base 10 and ln is the natural log.

Why do I see an error?

Check for a missing number, an unbalanced bracket or a division by zero.
