Pomodoro Timer

Focus in 25-minute sessions with short and long breaks and a chime between them.

Everything is worked out in your browser; nothing is sent anywhere or saved.

How to use

  1. Keep 25, 5 and 15 minutes, or set your own focus and break lengths.
  2. Press Start and work until the chime.
  3. Take the break, then carry on; Pause, Skip or Reset any time.

About Pomodoro Timer

The Pomodoro Timer splits work into focused blocks with planned rests: a short break after each focus session and a longer one after every fourth. The ring empties as time runs out, the tab title shows the minutes left, and a soft chime marks each change of phase. Time is measured against the clock, so it stays right even when the tab is in the background. It runs entirely in your browser, with nothing sent anywhere or saved.

FAQs

Why 25 minutes?

It is long enough to get real work done and short enough to stay sharp. Change it if a different length suits you.

Does it keep running in another tab?

Yes. Remaining time comes from the clock, not from counting ticks, so it stays accurate in background tabs.

Can I turn the chime off?

Yes. Use the sound button at the corner of the page to mute all sounds.
