Hashtag Suggestions

Up to 30 hashtags for your keyword, grouped into broad, niche and variants.

Everything runs in your browser; nothing you type is sent anywhere or saved.

How to use

  1. Type a keyword or two.
  2. Tap Generate to see broad, niche and variant tags.
  3. Copy all the tags, or one group, and paste them into your post.

About Hashtag Suggestions

Hashtag Suggestions builds tags from your keyword plus a small set of categories such as fitness, food, travel, tech and India, grouped as broad, niche and variants.

FAQs

Where do the ideas come from?

Ideas come from proven templates on your device, so they're instant and private.

How many hashtags should I use?

Instagram allows up to 30. A mix of broad and niche tags usually works better than only popular ones.

Do I need an account?

No. It's free with no sign-up, and nothing you type leaves your browser.
