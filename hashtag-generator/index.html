Hashtag Suggestions

Up to 30 hashtags for your keyword, grouped into broad, niche and variants.

Uses AI (Google Gemini). Template ideas are made in your browser. Only if you tap Generate with AI is the text you type sent to Toolsaku's server for the AI; nothing is saved and no files are uploaded.

How to use

  1. Type a keyword or two.
  2. Tap Generate to see broad, niche and variant tags.
  3. Copy all the tags, or one group, and paste them into your post.

About Hashtag Suggestions

Hashtag Suggestions builds tags from your keyword plus a small set of categories such as fitness, food, travel, tech and India, grouped as broad, niche and variants.

Hashtag Suggestions guide

How to pick hashtags that get reach

Mix three kinds: a few broad tags with millions of posts, several niche tags that describe exactly what your post is about, and one or two local tags such as your city. Niche tags face less competition, so your post stays visible longer.

Hashtags for Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok

Instagram allows up to 30 tags, but 5 to 15 relevant ones usually work better. YouTube shows the first three hashtags above the title, so put your most important ones first. On TikTok, three to five tags plus a clear caption is enough.

Avoid banned or spammy tags

Do not repeat the same block of tags on every post and skip tags that have nothing to do with your content. Change your set for each post using the generator's niche and variant groups.

FAQs

Where do the ideas come from?

The main generator uses proven templates on your device, so it's instant and private. Tap Generate with AI for fresh titles, captions, descriptions and hashtags in Hinglish or English, written by Google's Gemini AI.

How many hashtags should I use?

Instagram allows up to 30. A mix of broad and niche tags usually works better than only popular ones.

Do I need an account?

No. It's free with no sign-up. Template ideas never leave your browser; the AI button sends only the text you type, and nothing is saved.

Are these hashtags in Hindi too?

Type your keyword in Hindi or Hinglish and you get matching tags. The AI button can write Hinglish captions and tags for Indian audiences.

Should hashtags go in the caption or the first comment?

Both work on Instagram. Putting them in the caption keeps them attached if you edit the post later.

Does it work for YouTube?

Yes. Copy three to five tags into your description; YouTube shows the first three above your video title.
