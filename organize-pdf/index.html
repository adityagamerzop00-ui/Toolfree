PDF Page Organizer

Reorder, rotate and delete the pages of a PDF.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Only the open-source processing engine is downloaded when the tool opens.

How to use

  1. Add one PDF, up to 50 MB.
  2. Move pages with ← and →, rotate with ↻, or delete with ×.
  3. Tap Save PDF and download organized.pdf.

About PDF Page Organizer

PDF Page Organizer shows every page as a thumbnail so you can fix the order, turn sideways scans upright and drop pages you don't need. Pages are copied, not re-rendered, so text stays selectable and quality is unchanged. Your PDF is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

FAQs

Can I delete every page?

No. A PDF needs at least one page, so the last page can't be deleted.

Does rotating lower the quality?

No. Rotation is saved as a page setting, so the content isn't redrawn.

Can I undo a change?

Rotate a page four times to return it to its start, or tap Reset and add the PDF again to start over.
