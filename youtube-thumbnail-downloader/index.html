YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

Get the thumbnail images of any YouTube video in every size.

Thumbnails load straight from YouTube's public image server (img.youtube.com). Toolsaku stores nothing.

How to use

  1. Copy the link of a YouTube video, Short or live stream.
  2. Paste it (or the 11-character video ID) and tap Get thumbnails.
  3. Tap Download on the size you want, or open it full size and save it.

About YouTube Thumbnail Downloader

YouTube Thumbnail Downloader shows the thumbnail images YouTube publishes for a video: up to 1280 × 720, plus 640 × 480, 480 × 360 and 320 × 180. Sizes the video doesn't have are hidden. It works with watch, youtu.be, Shorts, embed and live links. Nothing is uploaded, and no sign-up is needed.

YouTube Thumbnail Downloader guide

How to download a YouTube thumbnail

Open the video in the YouTube app or website, tap Share, then Copy link. Paste the link here and tap Get thumbnails. You'll see every size YouTube has for that video, from 320 × 180 up to 1280 × 720 HD.

Works with Shorts and live streams

Regular videos, youtu.be short links, Shorts, embeds and live streams all work. You can also paste just the 11-character video ID.

Use thumbnails the right way

Thumbnails are handy for studying what gets clicks in your niche, for presentations, or for saving your own uploads. If you want to reuse someone else's thumbnail, ask the creator first.

FAQs

Why is the max resolution missing?

YouTube only makes a 1280 × 720 thumbnail for some videos, mostly HD uploads. Toolsaku hides sizes that don't exist.

Why doesn't Download appear?

Some browsers block direct downloads of the image. Tap Open full size, then long-press (phone) or right-click (computer) to save it.

Can I use the thumbnail?

Thumbnails belong to the video's creator. Use them for reference, or ask for permission before reusing them.

Can I download thumbnails of YouTube Shorts?

Yes. Paste the Shorts link and the tool finds its thumbnails.

Do I need to sign in?

No. There's no sign-up, and nothing is uploaded.

What size should my own thumbnail be?

YouTube recommends 1280 × 720 pixels (16:9). Use the Image Resizer's YouTube Thumbnail preset to get it right.
