Video Resizer

Change a video's resolution or reshape it for Reels, Shorts and TikTok.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Only the open-source processing engine is downloaded when the tool opens.

How to use

  1. Add one MP4, MOV, WEBM or MKV video, up to 500 MB.
  2. Pick a size such as 720p, Square or Vertical, and Contain or Cover for new shapes.
  3. Tap Resize video, then download the MP4.

About Video Resizer

Video Resizer re-encodes your video at a new size as an MP4. The 1080p to 360p presets set the short side, keep the shape of the picture and are greyed out when they'd be larger than your original. Square 1:1 and Vertical 9:16 make clips ready for social feeds. Your video is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

FAQs

Why are some sizes greyed out?

Making a video larger than its original resolution doesn't add detail, so those presets are turned off.

Contain or Cover?

Contain shows the whole picture with black bars. Cover fills the frame and trims the edges.

How long does it take?

SugoiTools uses your device's hardware encoder, so it's usually faster than real time.
