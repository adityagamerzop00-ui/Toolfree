SIP Calculator

How to use

  1. Enter your monthly SIP amount in ₹.
  2. Enter the annual return you expect and how many years you will invest.
  3. See the invested amount, estimated returns and total value.

About SIP Calculator

SIP Calculator assumes you invest at the start of each month and returns compound monthly: FV = P × ((1 + i)ⁿ − 1) ÷ i × (1 + i), where i is the annual rate ÷ 12 and n is the number of months. Real fund returns vary year to year. Everything is worked out in your browser as you type; nothing is sent anywhere or saved.

SIP Calculator guide

How a SIP grows

A SIP invests a fixed amount every month, and returns compound on everything you've invested so far. ₹5,000 a month for 10 years is ₹6,00,000 invested; at an assumed 12% a year it grows to roughly ₹11.6 lakh.

The power of time

Time matters more than the amount. The same ₹5,000 a month for 20 years instead of 10 can grow to roughly four times as much at the same return, because the gains keep compounding.

Use realistic returns

Equity mutual funds don't give a fixed return; some years are negative. Try a few rates, such as 8%, 10% and 12%, to see a range rather than one number.

FAQs

Are these returns guaranteed?

No. Mutual fund returns depend on markets; treat the result as an estimate only.

What return should I use?

Pick a conservative figure. Past returns don't promise future ones.

Does it include tax or exit load?

No. The result is before taxes, charges and exit loads.

Is SIP better than a lump sum?

A SIP spreads your buying over time and builds a saving habit. A lump sum can grow more if markets rise, but it carries more timing risk.

Is the SIP calculator accurate?

The maths is exact for a steady return. Real funds go up and down, so treat the result as an estimate.

Does it include step-up SIP?

Not yet. It assumes the same amount every month.
