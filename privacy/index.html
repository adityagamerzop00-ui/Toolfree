Privacy Policy

Your files

Files you open in Toolsaku (images, PDFs and videos) are processed locally in your browser and are never uploaded to our servers or anyone else's. When you close or reload the page, they are gone. The one exception is AI mode in Long Video to Shorts, described below, which sends small snapshots, never the file.

Long Video to Shorts: AI mode

Normal mode runs fully on your device. If you choose AI mode, your full video still stays on your device: only a few small, low-resolution snapshots (about 8 JPEG images, at most 360 pixels wide) and the spoken words, if the tool already has captions, from each loud moment are sent straight from your browser to Google's Gemini AI, using the Gemini API key you add, to check whether the moment is a real highlight. They never pass through Toolsaku's server. Your key is saved only in your browser's local storage, is sent only to Google, and you can remove it at any time; Google's Gemini API terms apply to what is sent with it. If you choose AI subtitles, the clip's audio is sent the same way, in short pieces of about one minute, so Gemini can write the captions; if that fails, captions are made on your device instead. When you export an AI clip, a short text summary of the moment (its hook title, reason and any title you typed) is sent the same way to write the post kit. Your IP address is held in memory for 10 minutes only to limit how often the post kit can be used.

AI Stream Moment Finder

This tool sends only the YouTube link you paste. Our server reads that video's public data on YouTube (title, chat replay, captions and the Most replayed graph; the video itself is never downloaded) and sends a short text summary of it to Google's Gemini AI to rank the moments. No files are uploaded, no account is needed, and the link is not tied to you. Results are kept in memory for 30 minutes so a repeat search is instant, and your IP address is held in memory for 10 minutes only to limit how often the tool can be used.

Data we collect

You can use every tool without an account, and Toolsaku does not ask for personal information to do so. Your theme and language choices are remembered in your browser's local storage and never leave your device. Toolsaku itself sets a cookie only if you sign in to the optional account described below. The ads on this site come from Google, which may set its own cookies (see Advertising).

Advertising

Toolsaku shows ads from Google AdSense to keep every tool free. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technology to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites, and to measure them. Your files are never shared with advertisers. You can turn off personalised ads in Google's Ad Settings, and read how Google uses data at policies.google.com.

Optional account and connected apps

The account is optional. It is only used if you tap Connect YouTube, Connect Instagram or Connect Facebook, so you can post a clip from the Long Video to Shorts Clipper straight to your own channel or page. Sign-in is with Google only; Toolsaku never sees or stores a password.

What we store on our own server (not shared with anyone):

  • From Google sign-in: your Google account ID, email address and name, so we can recognise you next time.
  • A login cookie (tsk_sid, HttpOnly, Secure) that keeps you signed in for up to 60 days. Only a scrambled (hashed) copy is kept on the server.
  • For each account you connect: its name (for example your channel email, Instagram username or Facebook Page name) and the access tokens the platform gives us. Tokens are stored encrypted and are used only when you tap Post.

Permissions we ask for: YouTube – upload videos (youtube.upload). Instagram – publish Reels to your professional account (instagram_basic, instagram_content_publish). Facebook – list your Pages and post videos to them (pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement, pages_manage_posts). We never post anything unless you tap Post, and we do not read your messages, followers or other content.

Videos you post: when you tap Post, that one clip is sent from your device to our server and then to the platform you chose. Our copy is deleted as soon as the upload finishes or fails. Clips you only download never leave your device.

Google user data: Toolsaku's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. We do not sell this data, use it for ads, or share it with anyone.

Disconnect or delete: open Account in the header. Disconnect removes one connected account and its tokens and asks the platform to cancel our access. Delete my account removes your Toolsaku account, every connected account and every token straight away. Your videos already posted on YouTube, Instagram or Facebook stay there. You can also remove Toolsaku from your Google account permissions or Facebook settings ("Apps and websites"); when Facebook tells us, we delete your Instagram and Facebook connections automatically.

AI मोड (हिन्दी में)

Long Video to Shorts में AI सबटाइटल चुनने पर क्लिप की आवाज़ लगभग एक-एक मिनट के टुकड़ों में आपकी अपनी Gemini key से सीधे Google को जाती है, हमारे सर्वर से नहीं। आपकी पूरी वीडियो फ़ाइल आपके डिवाइस पर ही रहती है।

वैकल्पिक अकाउंट (हिन्दी में)

सभी टूल बिना अकाउंट के मुफ़्त चलते हैं। अकाउंट तभी बनता है जब आप "YouTube / Instagram / Facebook जोड़ें" दबाते हैं, ताकि क्लिपर से क्लिप सीधे आपके चैनल पर पोस्ट हो सके। लॉगिन सिर्फ़ Google से होता है, हम कभी पासवर्ड नहीं देखते।

हम सिर्फ़ ये रखते हैं: आपका Google ID, ईमेल और नाम; एक लॉगिन कुकी; और जोड़े गए अकाउंट का नाम व उसके टोकन, जो एन्क्रिप्ट करके रखे जाते हैं। पोस्ट करते समय क्लिप हमारे सर्वर से होकर जाती है और काम पूरा होते ही मिटा दी जाती है।

हटाने के लिए: हेडर में अकाउंट खोलें। "हटाएँ" से एक अकाउंट हटता है, "मेरा अकाउंट डिलीट करें" से सब कुछ तुरंत मिट जाता है। YouTube, Instagram या Facebook पर पोस्ट हुए वीडियो वहीं रहते हैं।

Third-party services

Some tools load open-source code from public content delivery networks when you open them: the PDF tools load pdf-lib and PDF.js from jsDelivr, the video tools load Mediabunny from jsDelivr or unpkg, and the QR Code Generator loads qrcode-generator from jsDelivr. These requests fetch code only; your files are not sent with them. The YouTube Thumbnail Downloader loads thumbnail images directly from YouTube's image servers (img.youtube.com) for the video you enter. Our server (and its hosting provider) keeps standard logs, such as IP addresses and pages requested.
