        How to use

        1. Add a long video (MP4, MOV or WEBM) or tap a preset such as Gaming or Podcast.
        2. Pick the clip length, how many clips and the 9:16 crop, then choose caption and title styles.
        3. Tap Make clips, keep the tab open, then download or share each Short.

        About Long Video to Shorts

        Toolsaku' Long Video to Shorts maker finds good moments in a long video and turns them into vertical 9:16 clips for YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and Moj. It can add auto captions (Whisper, running in your browser), a hook title, effects and background music, and builds a post kit with titles, a description and hashtags. Everything is rendered on your device, so your video is never uploaded and there is no watermark.

        FAQs

        Is my video uploaded anywhere?

        No. The clips are cut and rendered in your browser. Only the open-source caption model and render engine are downloaded the first time you use them.

        Which browsers work?

        The latest Chrome or Edge on a computer or Android phone works best. Keep the tab open while clips are being made.

        Can it make captions in Hindi?

        Yes. Captions can be in Hindi, English or Hinglish, and the tool's own buttons switch to Hindi too.
