Merge PDF

Combine several PDFs into one file, in the order you choose.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Only the open-source processing engine is downloaded when the tool opens.

How to use

  1. Add two or more PDF files, up to 50 MB each.
  2. Use the arrows to set the order, or × to remove a file.
  3. Tap Merge PDFs and download merged.pdf.

About Merge PDF

Merge PDF joins several documents into a single file, keeping every page exactly as it was: text stays selectable and links keep working. It's handy for combining scanned pages, invoices or chapters before sending them as one attachment. Your PDF is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

Merge PDF guide

How to combine PDF files

Add your PDFs, put them in the order you want with the arrows, and tap Merge PDFs. The pages are joined exactly as they are, so nothing is re-compressed or blurred.

When merging helps

Many portals accept only one file: job applications, college admissions, visa forms and tax uploads. Merge your marksheets, ID and certificates into one PDF first, then compress it if the portal has a size limit.

Private by design

Merging happens in your browser, so bank statements, ID cards and contracts are never uploaded to a server.

FAQs

Is there a limit on files?

Each PDF can be up to 50 MB. There's no fixed number of files, but very large merges depend on your device's memory.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password in your PDF reader first, then add the file again.

Will the quality change?

No. Pages are copied as they are, without re-compressing images or text.

Can I merge PDFs on my phone?

Yes. It works in your phone's browser, with no app or sign-up needed.

Can I remove or reorder pages inside a PDF?

Use Organize PDF to reorder, rotate or delete pages, or Split PDF to pull out the pages you need.

Is there a watermark?

No. Merged files have no watermark and no page limit beyond your device's memory.
