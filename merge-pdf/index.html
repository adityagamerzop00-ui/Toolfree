Merge PDF

Combine several PDFs into one file, in the order you choose.

Your files are processed on your device and never uploaded. Only the open-source processing engine is downloaded when the tool opens.

How to use

  1. Add two or more PDF files, up to 50 MB each.
  2. Use the arrows to set the order, or × to remove a file.
  3. Tap Merge PDFs and download merged.pdf.

About Merge PDF

Merge PDF joins several documents into a single file, keeping every page exactly as it was: text stays selectable and links keep working. It's handy for combining scanned pages, invoices or chapters before sending them as one attachment. Your PDF is processed in your browser and is never uploaded.

FAQs

Is there a limit on files?

Each PDF can be up to 50 MB. There's no fixed number of files, but very large merges depend on your device's memory.

Can I merge password-protected PDFs?

No. Remove the password in your PDF reader first, then add the file again.

Will the quality change?

No. Pages are copied as they are, without re-compressing images or text.
